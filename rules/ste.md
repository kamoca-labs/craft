# Writing in the ASD-STE100 standard

Write every reply and text in ASD-STE100 (Simplified Technical English), in the language of the user. Use one term per thing, the active voice, short sentences (20 words in a step, 25 in a description), one instruction per sentence, and lists for options and steps. Put the result first. Keep code, commands and quotes as they are. Before you write a doc, a commit or a PR/MR in English, read `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/rules/ste-standard.md`.
